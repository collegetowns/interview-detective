Interview Detective

An activity builder for research methods classes: students read a study’s clues, reconstruct its interview protocol, and get an automatic score.

STEP 1Add one or more case files: a study’s abstract, research questions and findings as clues, plus its real interview protocol as the answer key.
STEP 2Preview the activity exactly as students will see it, and try writing a few questions yourself.
STEP 3Download the student activity: one HTML file to post in your learning platform or send to students.

How it works
  • Students choose a case, study the clues, and write the interview questions they think the researchers asked.
  • “Ready to Compare” reveals your answer key beside their questions, with arrows linking similar ones.
  • The score (0–100) is automatic: 80% how much of the real protocol their questions cover (matching distinctive words and common synonyms), 20% whether their questions are open-ended.
  • Students can save a typewritten “case report” image to submit.
  • Your work saves automatically in this browser. To edit a finished activity later, use Open an activity and choose its downloaded HTML file.

Everything you enter is built into the file you download, including abstracts and images, so use material you have the right to share with your students (for example, open-access articles).
