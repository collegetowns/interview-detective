How was my score calculated?

Your whole protocol is compared with the researchers’ whole protocol:

  • Coverage (80%): how much of the real protocol your questions cover, judged by key ideas (e.g. “barriers,” “parents,” “transfer to other areas of life”; synonyms count) and shared wording. Warm-up and closing questions count half.
  • Interview craft (20%): open-ended questions (How / What / Why / Describe / Tell me…) earn full credit; yes/no questions and questions that ask two things at once earn less.

The scoring is automatic and approximate. It matches ideas and words, not meaning, so a good question phrased unusually may deserve more credit than it gets. Questions without an arrow may still be excellent; discuss them.
